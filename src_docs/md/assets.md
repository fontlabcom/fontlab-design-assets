# Assets

Every asset lives in the repository under
[`src_docs/assets/`](https://github.com/fontlabcom/fontlab-design-assets/tree/main/src_docs/assets).
Browse a pack, then use the file directly or grab the raw download from GitHub.
The whole set is 169 files: logos, banners, app icons, and marketing screenshots.

## Company brand — `fontlab-marketingpack-company`

The FontLab Ltd. wordmark, in every form a layout will ask for.
[Browse the pack.](https://github.com/fontlabcom/fontlab-design-assets/tree/main/src_docs/assets/fontlab-marketingpack-company/fontlab-logo)

- **Logo font** — `fontlabltd-logo.otf` plus its `.vfj` source, so the wordmark stays editable in FontLab.
- **Vector** — Illustrator source (`.ai`) and print-ready PDFs: solid black, 25/50/75% black, and white for dark backgrounds.
- **Raster** — PNG wordmarks from 50px to 1024px, in black and the three gray tints, for slides and the web.

## Product banners — `fontlab-banners`

Web banners for the FontLab "series couple" campaign, sized 728×90.
[Browse the pack.](https://github.com/fontlabcom/fontlab-design-assets/tree/main/src_docs/assets/fontlab-banners)

- Four themes: `dark`, `graydark`, `graylight`, `light`.
- Each theme ships as PDF (print) and PNG (screen).

## FontLab 8 — `fontlab-marketingpack-fontlab-8`

App identity and store collateral for FontLab 8.
[Browse the pack.](https://github.com/fontlabcom/fontlab-design-assets/tree/main/src_docs/assets/fontlab-marketingpack-fontlab-8)

- App icons, full and simplified (`app-icon`, `app-icon-simple`).
- Hero and slide headers (`hero-head`, `slide-head`) and product screenshots.
- `fontlab-8-text-suggestions.txt` — approved copy for store listings.

## TransType 4 — `fontlab-marketingpack-transtype-4`

App identity and store collateral for TransType 4.
[Browse the pack.](https://github.com/fontlabcom/fontlab-design-assets/tree/main/src_docs/assets/fontlab-marketingpack-transtype-4)

- App icon, screenshots, and composed screenshot layouts (`screenshot-comps`).
